## Threats to validity

- 100 movies and 27 judged queries: differences between methods are within noise unless the intervals above exclude zero.
- Most pooled candidates are poor matches in a 100-movie corpus, so absolute scores are low for every method.
- Labels come from a first pass by an AI assistant and are validated on a random subset by humans (see the agreement line above); both can be wrong, and an AI judge may prefer semantically plausible matches.
- The design of v2 was informed by documented failures on these same queries (negation, typos, vague queries); the 50 dev queries (`../../evaluation/dev_queries.json`) were written separately for tuning, so this sample result is not an untouched test.
- Vague queries have no criteria; they are scored 1 for every film and reported separately.
